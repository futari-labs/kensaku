## Notes on behavior

- A finished run with no usage part and no error (and no failed request) is cancelled: every completed run ends with a usage part and every failed one with an error part. That keeps the notice after the next question and after a reload.
- A failed request (HTTP 400/409) or a broken stream is recorded as an assistant error message, so it is saved and restored like any run's error.
- The market selector sits outside the prompt's form: Radix Select inside a form resets itself to its first value when the form resets on submit.
