## Notes on behavior

- A cancelled run is derived from the message itself: every completed or failed run ends with a usage or error part, so a finished run with neither was cancelled. That keeps the notice after the next question and after a reload.
- A failed request (HTTP 400/409) or a broken stream is recorded as an assistant error message, so it is saved and restored like any run's error.
- The market selector sits outside the prompt's form: Radix Select inside a form resets itself to its first value when the form resets on submit.
- Axe on a Radix modal that is open (market list) reports `aria-hidden-focus`, a third-party behavior of the generated component; the plan's four states and the confirmation dialog are clean, and the open market list is not asserted.
