## Revisit triggers

Any of these justifies a separately reviewed plan revision; none of them may be handled by editing frozen files during execution:

- The live acceptance suite (EV-008) shows fewer than 4 of 6 queries with at least one valid merchant offer, which would mean D-011 does not deliver enough offers.
- Exa changes or removes the `summary` schema, `/answer` `outputSchema`, or free-plan terms, or a request is billed against a payment method.
- The owner wants deployment, more users, currency conversion, or a non-Exa provider.
- An AI Elements or shadcn component cannot meet an accessibility requirement without modification (D-018).
