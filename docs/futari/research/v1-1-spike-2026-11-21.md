# Research note: kensaku-v1-1 pre-approval spike (2026-11-21)

Purpose: before freezing plan `kensaku-v1-1`, test the Singapore fix, the extended `/answer` schema, and the reliability of Exa's `image` field with real data. Owner approval: AskUserQuestion, 2026-11-21, "Yes, ≤10 requests" (D-028).

Method:

- `spikes/exa-v1-1/spike.mjs` made 10 live Exa requests (cap 10, persisted counter), all HTTP 200, reported cost $0.11 from free credits: 8 before the independent review and 2 after it (V9, V10). The key was read from the owner's untracked `.env.local` through `EXA_ENV_FILE` and never copied; the script refuses to save a response that contains the key. Responses are frozen as `evals/fixtures/exa-live/V1`-`V10`.
- `spikes/exa-v1-1/image-probe.mjs` (no Exa use) fetched every distinct `image` URL in the spike and MVP search fixtures the way the planned proxy would (no cookies, 8 s timeout, 5 MiB cap, at most 3 redirects), sniffed magic bytes and dimensions, and applied the proposed URL rule. Image bytes were saved only to a temporary directory outside the repository and are not committed.
- `R1-Q5-offers-xm5` and `R1-Q6-offers-paperwhite` are byte-for-byte copies of two search responses from the EV-008 run 1 log (verified with `deepStrictEqual`).

## Consequences for the plan

- D-033: market-aware offer query template (V-F1 to V-F3).
- D-031 and D-032: classification rule additions and currency symbol mapping, frozen as EV-011 cases from V-F4 and V-F5.
- D-037: extended `/answer` schema with the price instruction (V-F6), the tighter identifier description (V-F7, V-F12), and the malformed-reason rule (V-F12). Remaining identifier weakness (V-F13) is risk R-21; prices in reasons (V-F13) are risk R-20.
- D-029, rules I1 to I3, and EV-013/EV-014/EV-016: image selection and proxy validation (V-F8 to V-F11). Rule I3's aspect limit is 1.8 (V-F14), which rejects the Logitech logo card. Other wrong images are measured by the owner's image audit in EV-016 (risk R-17).

Total live use before approval for plan `kensaku-v1-1`: 10 requests, $0.11. Combined with the MVP (19 requests, $0.205 during execution; 12 requests, $0.118 in the MVP spike), all from free credits.
